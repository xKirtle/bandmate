# Line-level Cues go dormant with their Alternate instead of being cleared

Supersedes the Alternate bullet of ADR 0005. Switching a Section's active Alternate used to clear the line-level Cues on its Occurrences. Now they stay attached to their Lines and lie dormant while that Alternate is inactive, then take effect again when it's switched back. The inactive Alternate's Lines are kept anyway, so keeping their Cues costs nothing. Clearing them made an Alternate switch lossy: trying a different version of a verse and switching back would throw away the timing you had synced. That would have needed undo or a Snapshot to recover. A newly activated Alternate still starts with no line-level Cues~~, and its Occurrences fall back to their Occurrence-level Cue, which is stored in its own right and never depends on a Line~~. _(Occurrence-level Cues are gone since ADR 0009: until its first Line is cued, such an Occurrence has no Cue.)_ _(A new Alternate made with ⇄ is the exception: since #180 it's made active as a copy of the active one, and starts with a copy of that one's Cues, which keeps its own, now dormant.)_ _(An inactive Alternate moved to the Scrapbook drops its dormant Cues, since #181: its new Section has no Occurrences for them to belong to.)_

_(Since ADR 0010, Cues live on Lines, not Occurrences, and an Alternate moved to the Scrapbook or into another Section keeps its dormant Cues instead of dropping them.)_
