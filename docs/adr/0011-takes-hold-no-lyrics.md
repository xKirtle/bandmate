# Takes hold no lyrics

Supersedes ADR 0004. A Take was going to keep a frozen copy of the lyrics active when it was recorded, so an old Take could still say which words it sang after the Lyric Sheet changed. Now a Take is only audio on the Timeline, like a Beat, and the only link between the Lyric Sheet and the Timeline is Cues, which tie Lines to times. What a Take sings is whatever Lines are cued over its span, now. A Take recorded before the lyrics changed simply goes out of date, which step 4 already accepted for the highlight, and Snapshots are where old wording is found. Keeping a second, Take-shaped link to the lyrics would have meant deciding how much to copy (the whole Arrangement, or only the Lines cued inside the Take, which is nothing before Cues exist) and a place to show it, for a question that rarely comes up. It can't be added back for Takes recorded without it.
