# Only Device Settings follow other tabs; what's remembered doesn't

A Device Setting (the Palette, light or dark, the Input, the Latency Offset, left-handed Chord diagrams) is a choice made on purpose, so a change made in one of a Device's tabs shows in every other tab at once. So does the Lyric Size. What's simply remembered from last time doesn't follow other tabs: the volume, a Song's Transpose, whether its Chords show, the Chord Chart's choices, preferred Voicings, the Chord Finder's tuning and Key, the Mixdown's format, the shift step, the Chosen Track and the Timeline's height. Each is read when it's needed and kept when it changes, and the last tab to change it wins.

Making them follow other tabs would mean moving about ten small, tested read-and-keep modules onto the Device Setting module, with a variant keyed per Song or per tuning, plus a storage listener for each. Bandmate has one user, and two of its tabs are rarely open at once, so the gain is a second tab not showing a stale value until it's reloaded. That isn't worth it. Architecture reviews have suggested this twice; it's declined until a remembered value going stale in another tab causes a real problem.
