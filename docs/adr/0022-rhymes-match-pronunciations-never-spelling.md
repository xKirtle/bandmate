# Rhymes match pronunciations, never spelling

A Rhyme is matched on how words sound, so every Language Bandmate rhymes in comes with a pronouncing dictionary: each word's phones, with its stress. Portuguese used to be read by spelling rules, which can't tell an open vowel from a closed one, so "depois" and "heróis" came up as perfect Rhymes. Portuguese (Portugal) now has a pronouncing dictionary. eSpeak NG works out a pronunciation for each word of the Hunspell word list once, when the dictionary is built. Wherever Wiktionary has the word, its hand-written stressed vowel overrides eSpeak's, and a small hand-checked file corrects the rest. A list of word pairs that must and mustn't rhyme checks the result. The spelling rules, and the support for them, are gone. A word the dictionary lacks gets no Rhymes rather than a guess.

No ready-made European Portuguese dictionary with stress exists. WikiPron's published Wiktionary data and the Montreal Forced Aligner's dictionaries have no stress, and ipa-dict has no European Portuguese at all. That's why the dictionary is generated and then corrected. Rescraping Wiktionary with stress kept would be more accurate, but it means maintaining a scraper. Keeping the spelling rules as a fallback for unknown words would bring their wrong vowels back for exactly those words. The cost is a bigger download, a few megabytes for 267k words with their pronunciations, and eSpeak NG is needed only by whoever rebuilds the dictionary. Going back to spelling rules would mean rebuilding both the data and the lookup.
