# place of those eSpeak NG and Wiktionary give: "word PHONES", one a line,
# in the phone set phones.go names. Each is the word's usual pronunciation,
# as it's sung; a word spelt the same as another is given the one songs use
# most. go generate ./internal/languagedata applies them.
