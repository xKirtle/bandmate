The guitar samples in these folders, one folder per string named by its open
note in standard tuning (E2, A2, D3, G3, B3, E4), are recordings of a
Raimundo 118 nylon-string classical guitar, played by Brian Penkrot in 2011,
from the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios' Musical Instrument
Samples (https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISguitar.html).

University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios, Musical Instrument Samples
(Raimundo 118, Brian Penkrot, 2011), trimmed, retuned and re-encoded.

The University of Iowa's Musical Instrument Samples page
(https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html) says of its recordings: "Since
1997, these recordings have been freely available on this website and may be
downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions."

They were taken from Iowa's mono mf recordings of each string (the A
string's F#3 from its ff recording), at frets 0, 3, 6, 9 and 12 of each
string. Modified: each note cut from Iowa's scales from its pluck, retuned to
A440, its level matched to the others, cut to its first 4.5 seconds with a
0.3 second fade out, and re-encoded mono, 44.1 kHz, MP3 at 96 kb/s, by
web/scripts/guitar-samples.mjs in Bandmate's source
(https://github.com/xKirtle/bandmate).
